Speak, move, and clap visibly. Finish recording and press Play. Check motion, orientation, and whether sound matches the clap.

Manual checks
  1. Start camera only, then camera + microphone; allow permission, record, finish, and play back.
  2. Rotate the phone, try front/back cameras, and apply a different video size or FPS.
  3. Stop camera only; audio should continue. Restart, then stop microphone only; video should continue.
  4. Clone and stop the original; the clone should still work. Stop all; device indicators should turn off.
  5. Revoke permission or remove the app using the toolbar. Capture should stop, including after a pending permission prompt.
  6. Try background/foreground and a longer recording. Note freezes, gaps, or growing audio/video offset.
  7. On the phone, compare Wi-Fi and cellular using the same HTTPS test URL.
